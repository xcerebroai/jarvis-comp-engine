# Jarvis Comp Engine

Property analysis for single-family real estate investors.

Enter a property address, add repair information and seller details, and review comparable sales, a conservative after-repair value, repair estimates, and offer options.

## Property research

[Open Jarvis Comps](https://comps.xcerebro.ai) to research a property and evaluate comparable sales.

## Acquisition workflow

1. Enter the property address.
2. Add the repair scope and any known property details.
3. Include seller, mortgage, and debt information when available.
4. Review comparable sales, the ARV range, repair estimates, and deal risks.
5. Compare wholesale, fix-and-flip, subject-to, and creative-finance offer options.
6. Save the analysis or copy the deal memo for your research records.

## Understanding your analysis

- **Comparable sales:** Sale dates, prices, property characteristics, and distance inform each comp's relevance.
- **Conservative ARV:** Qualified comps drive the recommended value; external estimates provide supporting context.
- **Repairs:** Estimates reflect the repair scope and property details you enter. Confirm the scope and costs with qualified contractors.
- **Offer options:** Strategy-specific estimates account for acquisition, repair, financing, and holding assumptions.
- **Data sources:** Each analysis identifies its underlying sources and available records. Coverage varies by location.

## Research guidelines

Property values and offer calculations are estimates, not appraisals or financial, legal, tax, or investment advice. Verify source records, property condition, title, financing terms, and other material facts before making an offer.

Automated property records come from licensed data providers. The application does not scrape restricted listing or MLS websites.
